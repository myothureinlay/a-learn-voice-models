A-Learn Mandarin voice pack, based on Kokoro-82M v1.0 and sherpa-onnx.
The base voices and model were created upstream. A-Learn modified the duration projection on 2026-11-19.
Kokoro model: https://huggingface.co/hexgrad/Kokoro-82M (Apache-2.0).
ONNX conversion and pack: https://github.com/k2-fsa/sherpa-onnx (Apache-2.0).
Chinese lexicon generated from Misaki (Apache-2.0) and pypinyin (MIT):
https://github.com/hexgrad/misaki and https://github.com/mozillazg/python-pinyin
Kokoro's training-data acknowledgments: Koniwa tnc (CC BY 3.0),
https://github.com/koniwa/koniwa, and SIWIS (CC BY 4.0),
https://datashare.ed.ac.uk/handle/10283/2353. Their recordings are not included.
Normalization data source: https://huggingface.co/csukuangfj/icefall-tts-aishell3-vits-low-2024-04-06
The source inputs for these normalization FST files have not been independently audited.

时长层微调使用北京希尔贝壳科技有限公司发布的 AISHELL-3 普通话语料及 PaddleSpeech 的时间对齐标注。

A-Learn experimental Mandarin prosody revision, November 2026.
Modifications to Kokoro-82M v2-pack weights: supervised adaptation of the final
pitch projection and a further update to the duration projection. Pitch targets
derive from AISHELL-3 human recordings measured with Praat via Parselmouth.
Duration targets derive from PaddleSpeech's AISHELL-3 alignments.
A-Learn 于 2026 年 11 月以 v2 语音包为基础试验性微调了基频和时长层。
基频取自 AISHELL-3 真人普通话朗读，时长监督使用 PaddleSpeech 的时间对齐标注。
